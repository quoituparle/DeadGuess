DAG002 — Engaged holder resolution

Closed as dead, by Expert's judgment on 2026-11-01. Value is ruled out for a long basket of broad initial Schedule 13D subjects, entered at quarter ends with filer type read from index names. Genuine activism identified from filing text was not tested, so its value is unknown. This is a failed direction at that scope, and nothing here is independent validation.

The Guess held that a fund crossing 5% with intent to influence pays through outcomes that arrive over months. One round tested it on seven exposed quarter-end windows. Buying fund-like 13D subjects lost 6.04% a quarter against matched stocks after costs, positive in 0 of 7 windows, and compounded to -22.77% against +30.12% for SPY. Gross excess was negative at every horizon, so there was no positive effect to decay.

Corrections to the study report

Subject identity. The frozen rule took the single master row in the universe, so a listed filer could become the subject when the true subject sat outside it, as with JPM on a BlackRock municipal fund or DIS on FuboTV. An audit of cached indexes, with no returns joined, flags 7 of 240 V0 positions and 36 of 195 V1 positions. The flag can err both ways. V0 is barely touched: the rest of the basket could reach zero gross only if flagged names lost 178% a quarter, which is impossible. V1 is not a clean placebo, so the comparison between V0 and V1 means little.

Whether the loss is real. The study report says the shortfall is not an artifact of the comparison, and that is too strong. The candidate's -5.20% sits below all 200 random baskets. However, the median name trails by only 2.45%, 3 of 7 positive, and an equal-weight benchmark penalizes any basket that misses lottery-like winners. The random draw was not stratified by cell. This remains unresolved. It does not change the long decision, because the basket fails at the mean and at the median, but it means no reliable short signal is shown here.

Other limits. Blank-check exclusion used the latest SIC, a small unmeasured look-ahead. Filer labels hold activists alongside family vehicles, strategic investors and deal buyers. Matching omits industry, market value and valuation. Prices are quarter-end close proxies without dividends. All data was already exposed. External requests: 0.

Problem

The label is crude: filer type comes from name words, so activists sit with family vehicles and deal buyers. The subject rule let listed filers count as subjects, flagging 7 of 240 V0 positions and 36 of 195 placebo positions, so the placebo is not clean. Whether the loss is a real effect or a benchmark artifact is unresolved, and it does not change the long decision.

Next

None for this Guess. A study of stated intent from filing text would be a new Guess naming DAG002 and needs new SEC requests. Four cached tests that could separate a real loss from a benchmark artifact are in expert response, and they matter only if short accounting is built.

Validity

  • Timing and matching look reasonable locally. Code excludes formation-day filings and amendments, excludes both event groups from controls, and builds matching cells from formation dollar volume and prior returns. Matching does not establish causal comparability.
  • Subject identification is materially flawed. The sole universe CIK is assumed to be the subject, allowing a listed filer to be selected instead. The audit flags 7/240 V0 and 36/195 V1 name-cohort observations. Flags are imperfect, not confirmed inversions; nevertheless, V1 is a weak placebo and G5 carries little weight. The correction’s numerical claim that contamination cannot reverse V0 is not independently verifiable from supplied name-level returns. [correction record]([local path]), [inversion audit]([local path])
  • Look-ahead is present. read_issuers uses the last encountered SIC across cached submissions rather than formation-dated SIC. Correction 002 identifies coverage through 2026q3; its effect is unmeasured. [screening script]([local path])
  • The mechanism is only loosely measured. Broad name tokens, including “LLC,” do not verify fund status or influence intent. Quarter-end entry can lag filing by 92 days, and no resolution outcomes are measured.
  • Execution remains illustrative. Costs consistently charge 50–100 bp round trip to baskets and none to controls. Gross failure makes costs secondary, but close proxies without dividends, fills or account constraints cannot establish executable $10,000-account performance. Sixteen V0 missing observations receive flat treatment; the reported −100% stress worsens the mean to −12.72%.
  • Statistical claims should remain limited. Seven windows and overlapping longer-horizon cohorts provide little precision. The unstratified, unscripted post-result random-basket check does not establish that matching artifacts are absent.
