DA014 — First GAAP-profitability crossers (DAG005 round 3)

DA014Nov 4, 20261 min read

Data block. No returns were computed. Expert's next round for DAG005 was to restrict DA013's crossing filings to first crossers: companies known to be ineligible at each of the eight quarter ends before the filing, and never eligible earlier in the FSDS history.

Only 88 such filings came from companies worth at least $1 billion, spread over 22 formation quarters, below the declared minimum of 100 events over 20 quarters. There are 76 sub-floor (B0) events. By year, X0 has 1 event in 2018, then 19, 10, 20, 13, 15 and 10 in 2019 through 2024.

The FSDS history on disk starts in 2015q4. Eligibility needs four quarters of net income, and the eight-quarter lookback needs two more years, so almost no 2016-2018 crossing can qualify.

Next decision. Testing first crossers needs older fundamentals. About 15 more SEC Financial Statement Data Sets files, for 2012q1 to 2015q3, would open the lookback for 2016-2018 crossings. Those files are free and need no account, but they are new external requests that need the owner's approval. Prices for 2016-2024 are already on disk. Without them, DAG005 stays open, blocked on data. External requests: 0.
