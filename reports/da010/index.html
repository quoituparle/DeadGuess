DA010 — Timing and short side of cured late filers (DAG004 round 2)

DA010Nov 4, 20262 min read

Promising development lead, not Alpha. DA009's cured late filers sat at the 46.4th percentile of their cells in the quarter ending before the notice window, at the 43.7th in the notice quarter and at the 40.4th after the cure. The shortfall therefore builds with the event rather than predating it, and T1 passes against the 45.19 floor. The liquid-tier short spread at 3%/yr borrow is +24.0% a quarter and positive in 7 of 7 windows, so T2 passes. Both rest on few names, on seven exposed windows, and on a direction chosen after DA009's result.

Measure Result Required
T1 pre-notice pooled percentile 46.42 (70 name-windows, 5 formations; permutation p for at or below 0.144) at least 45.19
Notice-quarter percentile (diagnostic) 43.68 (106 name-windows, 6 formations; p 0.010) —
T2 liquid short spread, 3%/yr borrow +24.0% a quarter, 7 of 7 windows, 1-13 names a window above 0 in at least 5 windows
C0 short spread at 0.5%, 3% and 20%/yr borrow +11.8%, +11.2%, +6.9% a quarter; 7, 7 and 5 of 7 windows diagnostic
Break-even borrow for C0 about 48% a year diagnostic

Pre-notice percentiles by formation were 34.7, 46.3, 56.5, 44.3 and 47.7. That spread is wide, and 50 of 120 names were not in the universe two quarters earlier, mostly because they failed the price or liquidity floor then. So the timing evidence is weaker than its pass suggests: names that were already weak are underrepresented. After the cure, the shortfall narrows from 9.6 points below 50 at Q+1 to about 4 points by Q+4. That is a fade over months, but it is incomplete.

Limits. No short ledger, locate, recall, margin interest or payment in lieu of dividends is modelled. Late filers are often hard to borrow, and no historical borrow fee exists here. The liquid tier is a dollar-volume proxy without a market-cap floor, and one window has a single liquid name. External requests: 0.
