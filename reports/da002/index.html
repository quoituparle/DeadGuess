DA002 — Fund-filed initial Schedule 13D, quarter-end screen

DA002Nov 1, 20263 min read

The candidate fails clearly. Buying stocks after a fund-like initial 13D lost 6.04% a quarter against matched stocks after costs, with 0 of 7 windows positive. It compounded to -22.77% while SPY made +30.12% and the control universe +20.68%. The placebo of non-fund filers lost about the same, 5.92% a quarter, so the fund-versus-other distinction the mechanism needs did not appear. This tested long specification stops here.

The shortfall is not an artifact of the comparison. Two hundred random baskets of the same size drawn from the controls averaged +0.09% gross, with a 95% range of -3.40% to +6.04%; the candidate's -5.20% gross is at the bottom of that range. It is less severe at the median: the median name trails its cell median by 2.45% a window, with 3 of 7 positive. Part of the gap is therefore a missing right tail, as the controls include lottery-like winners that these baskets did not.

Diagnostics

Gross excess stays negative at every horizon: -5.20% at Q+1, -3.59% at Q+2, -1.23% at Q+3 and -4.22% at Q+4. There is no positive effect to decay. Thin names lose 7.18% a quarter and liquid names 2.22%. Subjects where the same filer earlier filed a 13G, the sharper signal of a passive holder turning active, show -3.95% gross, positive in 3 of 7 windows across 6 to 11 names. Filing age does not rescue it, at -4.84% within 30 days, -2.42% from 31 to 60 and -8.50% beyond 60.

Doubled costs give -6.88% a quarter. Charging -100% to the 16 of 240 positions missing at the next snapshot gives -12.72%. Holding them flat also ignores any takeover premium they earned, so that bound is one-sided. Pharma and biotech are only 10.4% of V0 against 9.4% of the universe, and removing them leaves -6.38% gross, so biotech financing is not the explanation.

The market-slope gate passed at 0.18, so the DA001 problem did not recur, but it was passing on a loss. All this inspection after the result is descriptive and changes no verdict.

Method and limits

Formations are the DA001 quarter ends, with 3,043 to 3,227 eligible names and 19 to 41 subjects. An event is an initial 13D filed in the 92 days before formation, with a subject in the liquid universe, listed at least about 270 days and not a blank-check company. Filer type comes from name words in the EDGAR index, and no filing text was read. That label is crude: it holds activists alongside family vehicles, strategic investors and deal buyers, whose stocks may be capped at a deal price. Matching uses dollar volume, not market value, and only one prior quarter of return. It also omits industry and valuation.

Prices are quarter-end close proxies without dividends. The latest window has only 19 subjects, after recent listings and blank-check companies removed many filings. The t-like ratio of -4.85 rests on seven windows and overlapping issuers, so it should not be read as a precise probability. Rank IC, funded NAV and Sharpe are not applicable. All data was already exposed. External requests: 0.

Next decision

This is a negative result for this long specification, not for genuine activism, which a name rule cannot isolate. A filing-text study of stated intent would need new SEC requests. A short version is not available because the engine has no short accounting. This is the second consecutive no-pass Batch on the Claude line, so expert consultation comes before any further Batch.
