DA015 — First GAAP-profitability crossers with history from 2012 (DAG005 round 4)

DA015Nov 4, 20261 min read

Data block. No returns were computed. I added the 15 owner-approved SEC Financial Statement Data Sets files for 2012q1-2015q3 (15 requests, all successful) to give 2017-2018 crossings their eight-quarter lookback.

The first-crosser count fell from 88 to 80 events among companies worth at least $1 billion, over 28 quarters. With history back to 2012, the never-eligible-before rule now also catches companies that were eligible in 2012-2015. That leaves about 10 true first crossers a year: 6, 8, 8, 9, 16, 11, 13 and 9 from 2017 to 2024. There are 72 sub-floor (B0) events.

The declared minimum is 100 events over 20 quarters, so the frozen stop rule ends the round before any return. More fundamentals cannot raise the count, because the population is small. A longer price history before 2016 is not on disk.

Next decision. DAG005 goes to Expert for a verdict. Its only remaining test is too thin to meet the minimum declared for it.
