DA004 — Confirmed fundamentals where price lagged (DAG001 round 2)

DA004Nov 4, 20264 min read

No pass. Top-quintile confirmed-improvement names whose prior-quarter return was below the median earned +1.09% a quarter over matched stocks after costs, with no market link (slope -0.04). But they were positive in only 4 of 6 windows against the required 5, and the t-like ratio is 0.51. The names whose price had already risen were no weaker (+1.89%), so the data gives no evidence either way that lagging price marks the underreaction. This is a failed screen on exposed development data, not Alpha.

DA001's broad top decile moved with the market. This round asked Expert's narrower question: if investors anchor on price trend, confirmed improvement should be underpriced where price has not yet acknowledged it, and it should beat stocks of the same liquidity and prior return. In matched cells L0 shows no market link, but H0 in the same cells keeps a slope of 0.46, so matching alone does not remove it. What remains for L0 is a small, unstable edge at the 51st percentile of the matched cell, and no difference by prior price can be shown.

Diagnostics

The within-cell permutation p for L0 is 0.080, but it treats names as independent; window percentiles vary far more than that allows, and a window-clustered t on G1 is about 0.44. Dropping 2026-06-30 turns its mean to -0.40%. Doubled costs leave +0.44% in 3 of 6 windows, and charging -100% to names missing at the next snapshot leaves +0.65% in 3 of 6. L0 puts 7.0% of name-windows in the top 5% of its cells and 7.6% in the bottom 5%. In cells of about 30 controls a chance name reaches either tail about 6.5% of the time, so both rates are near chance, and the 5% null in RESULTS.json overstates them. Liquid names (formation dollar volume of at least $20m) earned +2.00% in 4 of 6 windows, and thin names lost 1.66%.

L0's gross matched excess by horizon is +1.73% at Q+1 (6 cohorts), +2.41% at Q+2 (5), -0.60% at Q+3 (4) and -0.27% at Q+4 (3). Mean percentiles are 51.2, 51.1, 50.0 and 48.7. That is the right sign for a fade over months, but the later horizons rest on three or four cohorts. With DA002's coarser cells (dollar-volume decile by prior tercile), L0 is 51.73, +0.97% and 3 of 6. L0 minus H0 is -0.80% a quarter and positive in 2 of 6 windows.

Method and limits

DA001's frozen signal table supplies the scored names and V0, the minimum of the within-formation ranks of year-over-year revenue, operating income and operating cash-flow changes over assets, all from one original 10-K or 10-Q. The DA001 panel was rebuilt with the false MPTI stock-dividend record removed (DA001 CORRECTION_005). The rebuilt panel differs from DA001's only at MPTI's two affected entries. The top quintile was split at the scored median of split-adjusted prior-quarter return. Controls are the scored names outside the top quintile. Cells cross formation dollar-volume quintiles with prior-return deciles, both cut on the controls, and no cell was empty. Names and cells were frozen before any forward return was read.

Returns are quarter-end close proxies without dividends. Names missing at a later snapshot are held flat. Costs are a full round trip each quarter, at 25 bp per side above $20m of formation dollar volume and 50 bp below, with none on controls. Rank IC, funded NAV and Sharpe are not applicable to six quarterly marks of a conditional basket. The conditioning on prior return was proposed after DA001's results, so this is development reanalysis. A diagnostic binomial overflow was fixed before any output (control/CORRECTION_001.json). External requests: 0.
