DA011 — Cured late filers, short side, 2016-2024 replication (DAG004 round 3)

DA011Nov 4, 20263 min read

No pass. On unseen 2016-2024 history, cured late filers again rank below matched stocks, and the shortfall fades over months. But it was already there before the notice, and the borrowable names earn nothing.

Cured late filers sat at the 45.3rd percentile of their cells (t 3.4). The short spread after costs and 3%/yr borrow was +4.4% a quarter, market-adjusted (t 3.0), and positive in 22 of 30 windows. The shortfall was larger at Q+1 and Q+2 than at Q+3 and Q+4 (t 3.3).

The two failures:

  • S5: the same names already ranked at the 45.4th percentile in the quarter ending before the notice window, against a declared floor of 47.7.
  • S6: the borrowable tier lost 0.9% a quarter.

On the readings declared before the data, the shortfall is persistent selection rather than a month-scale event effect, and a short of large, easy-to-borrow names would not have paid. This is not Alpha.

One data defect matters. A repair rule I added before events were joined turned SIGM's 2018 liquidating distribution into a 25:1 implied split, so the distribution was credited 25 times over, worth +2,455%. Without the fix, S2 and S4 also fail. The fix (control/CORRECTION_006.json) was found by auditing the largest window outlier after results were read. Both versions are kept; S5 and S6 fail in both.

Gross matched shortfall by horizon was 5.8%, 3.0%, 0.7% and 3.4% a quarter for Q+1 to Q+4. The notice-quarter percentile was 46.3. The robustness checks held their sign:

  • excluding former SPACs: 47.2, +3.2%
  • own-prefix names only: +3.7%
  • -100% on control exits: +3.0%
  • -100% on C0 exits: 44.3, +6.0%

The C0 spread was +4.8% at 0.5%/yr borrow, 0.0% at 20%/yr and -5.1% at 40%/yr, with break-even near 20%/yr. Late filers are usually hard to borrow, so the thin names cannot carry a funded claim. By sub-period the spread was +3.2% for 2016-2019 and +4.9% for 2020-2024. Leaving out any one year keeps it between +3.5% and +4.9%. Uncured filers (U0) were near the median at 49.3, with a spread of -3.8%.

Method and limits

  • Data: EDGAR master indexes for 2015Q3-2023Q4 (34 SEC requests), plus cached 2024. Alpaca Basic SIP raw daily bars and every corporate-action type for 2015-10 to 2024-12: 1,396 requests within the 2,100 reserved, eight chunks re-fetched without escrow codes (CORRECTION_002).
  • Identity: DA008's I2. At 2024-12-31 on Alpaca bars it had precision 0.997 and recall 0.903.
  • Split audit: 393 implied splits, 11 cash-distribution moves kept as real, 79 records dropped, 1 re-dated, and 49 symbols (0.75%) unresolved, under the 1% line (CORRECTION_003, CORRECTION_006).
  • Shares: from balance-sheet or weighted-average counts, because FSDS omits cover-page counts (CORRECTION_004).
  • Coverage: 30 of 34 quarter ends had at least 15 cured names (803 name-windows), so the sign threshold was 20.
  • Exits and distributions: 206 spin-off distributions could not be priced and were valued at zero.
  • Not modelled: short ledger, locate, recall, margin interest and payments in lieu.
  • Status of the data: 2016-2024 is now exposed for DAG004.

Next decision

This specification stops. On the declared readings, S1-S3 passing with S5 failing reads as dead for this line, a persistent shortfall rather than month-scale event alpha. The failed S6 separately says the tradable tier earns nothing. DAG004 goes to Expert for its judgment.
