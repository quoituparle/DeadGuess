DA009 — Cured late filers, quarter-end screen (DAG004 round 1)

DA009Nov 4, 20262 min read

No pass, and the sign is the opposite of the premise. Names that filed an NT 10-K or NT 10-Q in the 92 days before a quarter end and then filed every late report lagged matched stocks by 11.9% a quarter after costs. The shortfall appeared in all 7 windows (t-like -4.3), and the names sat at the 40.4th percentile of their cells, with no market link (slope 0.03). The long premise, that the discount clears after the cure, is refuted on these windows. This is exposed development data, not Alpha.

The median cured name lagged its cell in every window, by 3.6% to 18.1%. So did the liquid tier, at 20 million USD or more of formation dollar volume (1 to 13 names). C0's gross matched excess was -11.0% at Q+1, -5.1% at Q+2, -8.0% at Q+3 and -2.0% at Q+4; its percentiles were 40.3, 46.6, 44.4 and 46.1. Doubled costs give -12.8%. A -100% bound on names missing at the next snapshot gives -16.2%. The permutation p for a percentile at or above the observed is 1.00.

Method. Events are NT 10-K and NT 10-Q rows in the cached EDGAR master indexes. A notice is cured when the matching 10-K, 10-KT or 10-Q is filed on or after the notice and by t-1.

Universe. DA002's rules, with point-in-time SIC to exclude blank checks: close at least $5, at least $100,000 of formation dollar volume, one security per CIK, and listed at least 270 days.

Cells and returns. Cells cross dollar-volume quintiles with prior-return deciles cut on the controls; 2024-12-31 uses dollar-volume quintiles only. Returns follow DA004: quarter-end close proxies and price returns. The event table was frozen before the plan and before any return was read. External requests: 0.

Next decision. The long specification stops here. The opposite side is DAG004's round 2 (DA010).
