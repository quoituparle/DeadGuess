DA012 — First GAAP-profitability crossers above the index size floor (DAG005 round 1)

DA012Nov 4, 20262 min read

No pass. Companies worth at least $1 billion that newly meet the S&P earnings rule earn nothing over matched stocks. The rule requires positive trailing four-quarter and latest-quarter GAAP net income. After costs these names earned -0.35% a quarter against stocks of the same liquidity and prior return, at the 50.9th percentile of their cells (t 1.2), in 14 of 30 windows. There was no market link (slope -0.06) and no fade.

The predicted index-demand drift is absent at quarter-end entry on 2,275 name-windows from 2017 to 2024. The only gate passed is C5, and only because the placebo did worse: crossers worth $100 million to $1 billion lost 3.1% a quarter (intercept t -2.5). This is not Alpha.

X0's gross matched excess by horizon was +0.31%, -1.48%, -0.07% and -1.11% for Q+1 to Q+4. B0's was -2.13%, +1.16%, -0.32% and -0.93%, at the 50.3rd percentile.

Method. Net income comes from FSDS: 10-Q quarters, plus Q4 derived as the 10-K annual figure minus three quarters. Each value is available from its filing's acceptance date. Eligibility is tested at each quarter end. A crosser is eligible now and known to have been ineligible one quarter earlier. 30 of 31 quarter ends from 2017-03-31 to 2024-09-30 had at least 15 X0 names (36 to 174). Market cap uses DA011's FSDS share counts. The universe, cells, return walker, corporate actions and costs are DA011's. The 2016-2024 price panel was already used for DAG004's cells, though no crossing return had been computed. External requests: 0.

Limits.

  • A "first" crossing here means ineligible last quarter, so it includes repeat crossers. The 2018-06-30 window (174 names) reflects the reversal of the tax-reform Q4 2017 losses.
  • The floor is a fixed $1 billion rather than the S&P index's changing threshold.
  • Index membership is not cached, so inclusions themselves are not observed.
  • Entry is at the quarter end after the crossing filing, up to three months late.

Next decision. This specification stops. The remaining question inside this Guess is timing: whether anticipating demand acts right after the crossing filing, before quarter-end entry.
